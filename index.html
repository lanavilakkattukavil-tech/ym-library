ABOUT OUR LIBRARY

The Yamaniyya English Medium School Library is a welcoming learning space designed to encourage reading, curiosity, creativity and independent learning among students.

Our collection includes story books, reference materials, educational resources, periodicals and books for different age groups.
All materials are systematically organized using the Dewey Decimal Classification(DDC)system.
Our modern Online Public Access Catalogue(OPAC) helps students and staff easily search,locate,and check book availability.
